Same window at 1:2. Dashed lines: the list and preview share a bottom line; the slots sit on the status strip.

Rules

  • Two bands right of the sidebar. The top band holds the profile fields, the item list and the preview corner; the list and the preview end on one line.
  • The settings row is three equal slots, win-gap-main apart, anchored to the status strip. Its height is the tallest group’s; the list and the preview take what is left, so Show advanced never grows the window.
  • Slots keep their place: a kind with no group for a slot leaves it empty, so the look always sits under the preview.
  • Three slots need 260 px each. Narrower, the preview hides, slots 1 and 2 stack in the first column, and Position on window moves into the look slot.
  • Tab follows reading order: fields, list, preview corner, then slots 1 to 3.
